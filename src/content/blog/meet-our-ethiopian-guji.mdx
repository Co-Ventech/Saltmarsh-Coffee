Our current single origin comes from smallholder farmers around Guji in southern Ethiopia, growing at around 2,000 metres.

## In the cup

Blueberry jam, jasmine and a honey sweetness that lingers. It is light and tea-like, so it shines as a filter coffee and works as a gentle, fruity espresso.

## How it is made

The cherries are picked ripe, washed and dried on raised beds. We pay well above the Fairtrade minimum, and the price per kilo is printed on the bag.

Try it in the [Sampler](/pricing) or as your monthly filter bag.
