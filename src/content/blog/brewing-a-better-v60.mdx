The V60 rewards patience more than gear. This is the recipe we use every morning.

## The recipe

| | |
|---|---|
| Coffee | 15 g, medium-fine grind |
| Water | 250 g at 94 °C |
| Time | about 3 minutes |

1. Rinse the paper filter and warm the server.
2. Add the coffee and pour 40 g of water. Let it bloom for 30 seconds.
3. Pour slowly in circles up to 150 g, then up to 250 g.
4. Let it drain, swirl and serve.

## If it tastes off

- **Sour or thin:** grind finer or pour slower.
- **Bitter or dry:** grind coarser or use slightly cooler water.
- **Flat:** use fresher beans, which is where our roast date helps.
