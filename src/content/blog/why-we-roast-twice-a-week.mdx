Coffee is at its best between a few days and a few weeks after roasting. Roast too far ahead and the aroma fades before the bag reaches you.

## Small batches, fixed days

We roast in 12 kg batches on **Tuesday** and **Friday**. Every order placed before the roast is packed the same day and posted by tracked courier, so most bags reach you within two days of leaving the roaster.

## What that means for you

- Beans that are still degassing, which is when flavour is at its sweetest
- A roast date printed on every bag, never a best-before date alone
- No stock sitting on a shelf waiting for an order
